## Tournament Format

WINIO CROWN SERIES consists of three competitive stages:

### Stage 1 — Open Qualifications

- Four Open Qualifications
- All matches are **Best-of-1 (Bo1)**
- Format: **Single Elimination**
- **Top 4 teams from each Open Qualification advance to the Main Event**
- Platform: **FACEIT**
- Total qualified teams: **16**

### Stage 2 — Group Stage

- Dates: **16–20 November 2026**
- Participants: **16 teams qualified through the Open Qualifications**
- Format: **4 GSL Groups**
- All matches are **Best-of-3 (Bo3)**
- **Top 2 teams from each group advance to the Playoffs**
- Total teams advancing: **8**

### Stage 3 — Playoffs

- Dates: **21–22 November 2026**
- Participants: **8 teams from the Group Stage**
- Format: **Double Elimination**
- All matches are **Best-of-3 (Bo3)**

## Main event seeding

- Initial Group Stage seeding will be based on the applicable **Valve Regional Standings (VRS)**.
- VRS-ranked teams will be seeded according to their VRS standing.
- Teams without a VRS ranking will be assigned to the remaining Group Stage seed positions randomly.
- Unranked teams will not receive a higher initial seed than VRS-ranked teams.
- **VRS publication used for seeding:** 2nd November 2026
